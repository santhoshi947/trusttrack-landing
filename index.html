The Problem

The Delivery Visibility Gap

Traditional courier tracking tells you where a package is located on a map, but leaves customers and brands completely blind to how it was handled or if it was tampered with in transit.

Package Uncertainty

When receiving laptops, jewelry, or electronics worth thousands, buyers cannot know if the contents are intact inside until after unboxing—making immediate dispute claims difficult.

Tampering & Swap Risk

High-value parcels pass through dozens of hands. Mid-transit package swapping, seal breaching, or subtle box opening remains undetectable with conventional cardboard taping.

Limited Accountability

When a delivery goes wrong, logistics providers and sellers blame each other. The absence of verifiable handling logs makes it hard to pinpoint exactly where damage occurred.

Simple Process

How TrustTrack Works

A seamless 4-step verification flow built to integrate easily with existing logistics pipelines.

1
SECURE

Package Sealed

High-value item is packaged with a TrustTrack tamper-evident seal and assigned a unique QR tracking identifier at dispatch.

2
SCAN

Checkpoint Logs

Logistics staff scan the QR code at each sorting hub. Seal status and handler details are logged instantaneously.

3
TRACK

Live Visibility

Buyer and seller receive real-time journey updates. Immediate warnings trigger if seal disruption is detected.

4
VERIFY

Final Verification

Before accepting, the customer scans the QR code on delivery to confirm 100% untampered seal status.
